# Curriculum Unlock Plan

## Goal
Use unlocks to increase lesson completion, reinforce skill identity, and make checkpoints feel materially rewarding.

## Reward Structure

### 1. Lesson Micro Unlocks
- Trigger: selected mid-module lessons
- Purpose: keep momentum high
- Best reward types:
  - 1 recipe card
  - 1 bartender hack card
  - 1 flavor pairing card
  - 1 hosting prep template

### 2. Module Checkpoint Unlocks
- Trigger: checkpoint lesson completion
- Purpose: mark meaningful progress
- Best reward types:
  - premium exclusive recipe
  - mini recipe pack
  - badge
  - practical reference sheet

### 3. Path Unlocks
- Trigger: full module completion
- Purpose: identity progression and future motivation
- Best reward types:
  - next-module preview recipe
  - badge tier
  - advanced branch teaser

## Recommended Unlock Inventory at Launch
- Total exclusive unlockable recipes: 24 to 36
- Target per major module: 3 to 4 recipes
- Checkpoint reward: 1 stronger exclusive per module
- Supporting non-recipe unlocks: 8 to 12 utility items total

## Valuable Exclusive Item Types

### Highest value
1. Exclusive recipes
- easiest for users to understand
- directly useful
- collectible
- emotionally rewarding

### Strong secondary items
1. Bartender hacks
2. Build sheets / spec cards
3. Flavor pairing matrices
4. Hosting prep templates
5. Zero-proof signature cards

## Unlock Timing Rules
- Never make every lesson unlock something. It will feel cheap.
- Best cadence:
  - one unlock around lesson 3 or 4
  - one unlock around lesson 7 or 8
  - one premium unlock at checkpoint
- Utility rewards should support the module's skill goal.
- Recipe rewards should demonstrate the skill the user just learned.

## Tier Strategy
- Free users: core module unlocks only
- PLUS users: core unlocks + Global Cocktail Culture rewards
- PRO users: all unlocks including Mastery rewards

## Product Recommendation
Start with recipes as the primary exclusive unlock.
They are the clearest value signal and the easiest thing for users to want, remember, and return to collect.

Use utility unlocks to support recipes rather than replace them.
