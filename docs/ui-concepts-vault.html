Production
Distilled from a mash of at least 51% corn to no more than 80% ABV, then aged in new, charred American oak barrels. The char layer filters congeners and draws out vanilla and caramel compounds from the wood.
Signature Pours
Old Fashioned
Manhattan

What to Look For
Proof: 90–100 is the cocktail sweet spot. Cask-strength (115–130+) is for neat sipping only.
Age statement: 4–6 years gives the best cocktail balance. 8+ starts to overpower everything else in the glass.
Mash bill: High rye (18%+) reads as spicy and dry. Wheated bourbons go softer with a longer, sweeter finish.
